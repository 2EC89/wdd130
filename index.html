BENIN REPUBLIC

Benin is a country in West Africa bordered by Togo, Nigeria, Burkina Faso, and Niger. It is known for its rich history, culture, traditions, lively markets, and beautiful beaches. Porto-Novo is the capital, while Cotonou is the main economic city.
