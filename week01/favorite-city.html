My Favorite City

My favorite City is Cotonou. Cotonou is my favorite city. Located along the beautiful coast of Benin, it is a vibrant and dynamic city where modern life meets authentic West African culture. I love its lively atmosphere, beautiful beaches, local markets, delicious food, and the warmth of its people. Cotonou has a unique energy, especially around places like the Dantokpa market, the waterfront, and the famous Route des Pêches. For me, it represents both the heart of Benin and a place where tradition, business, creativity, and everyday life come together.
